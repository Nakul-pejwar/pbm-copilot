# 7-minute demo

1. Start on the Superset dashboard.
2. Point out 100K synthetic claims processed.
3. Explain anomaly rate and high-risk queue.
4. Show the Provider Health (ClaimTrust) chart: providers rated 300-900, worst first - call out a Poor-rated provider.
5. Drill into a provider with unusual volume.
6. Show a high-risk claim and its triggered rules.
7. Open `/docs` and call `GET /claims/{claim_id}`.
8. Call `POST /explain/{claim_id}`.
9. Explain that GenAI does not decide the anomaly; it explains evidence generated by the detection layer.
10. Close with production roadmap: real claims ingestion, RBAC, audit, model governance and human review.
